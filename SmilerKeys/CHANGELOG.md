# SmilerKeys 0.13.0

- The wishlist now sits beside your character sheet: one box per slot with what you're wearing, what you want (with item level and source), and a BiS star. Slots turn green when you're wearing your BiS
- Click + on a slot to pick from the loot list for that slot, or drag an item from your bags onto the box
- Where to go (bonus rolls, farm, vault, crafting) is under the slots and follows your BiS marks
- Import: paste QuestionablyEpic Upgrade Finder or Raidbots Droptimizer results, or any Wowhead links / item IDs (/sk import)
- The loot list is now just the list: click an item to add it (/sk browse)
- With the Smiler add-on: exact Crystal Ball colours, Barlow fonts, and settings in the Smiler window (/smiler)

# SmilerKeys 0.12.0

- Fix: Battle.net friends who also have the Battle.net app open were skipped – SmilerKeys now finds their WoW character on any of their game accounts
- Fix: a friend-list entry with a blank name (not loaded yet) caused an error
- Fix: error after finishing a key
- Friends now swap data automatically when they log into WoW – no /sk sync needed
- New /sk debug: who you can reach, messages sent and received

# SmilerKeys 0.11.0

- Wishlist slots show BiS achieved or not – automatic when you wear your BiS items, or tick it yourself
- "Where to go" is now PLAN with tabs: Bonus rolls, Farm, Vault, Craft – ranked by BiS items, showing the item level each gives
- Slots marked BiS-achieved drop out of the plans

# SmilerKeys 0.10.0

- Bonus-roll and vault items show the real item you'd get – e.g. Coiled Altar and Ula'tek bonus rolled on Heroic come out at Myth 1/6 (318), not the higher Mythic track
- Tier pieces for your class under the bosses whose tokens give them, at each difficulty's item level
- Labyrinth of Kindo'jan Myth armaments (318) for every armour and jewellery slot
- Off-spec trinkets for your class, so healers see DPS trinkets like Twisted Horror's Tendril
- Best-in-slot marking: alt-click for a gold outline and star; BiS filter

# SmilerKeys 0.9.0

- Wishlist items remember how you'll get them – drop, bonus roll or Great Vault – plus the raid difficulty or key level, and show that item level
- New VIA filter so the list shows drop, bonus roll or vault item levels
- Crafted gear: this season's crafted epics and items embellished by default, filtered to what your class and spec can use
- Every embellishment reagent, with its effect and where it fits; the wishlist warns when you plan more than 2 embellishments
- Crafting orders appear in "Where to go"

# SmilerKeys 0.8.0

- New loot window (`/sk loot` or the Loot button): every item from this season's raids at all four difficulties and M+ dungeons, filterable and sortable
- Item levels for your chosen raid difficulty or keystone level, with the gain over your equipped item
- Wishlist: drag items onto your gear slots; "Where to go" shows which bosses and dungeons to target
- Bonus-rollable sources marked, with what a bonus roll gives there

# SmilerKeys 0.7.0

First public release.

- One grid of everyone's best timed Mythic+ key per dungeon, their current keystone, M+ score and Resilient Keystone level
- Shares automatically with your guild, character friends, Battle.net friends and group – no setup
- Group members without SmilerKeys still show their scores
- Alts grouped under their Battle.net account
- Soft gold glow on keys that would upgrade someone, and on the cells they'd upgrade
- Click a dungeon icon to cast its teleport
- Friends and group members you don't know are shown in separate sections
- Tells you when a friend is running a newer version
